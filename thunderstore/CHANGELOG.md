## 1.0.384

### Changed
- Rounded minimap setting added; the UI override and sky drift settings go back to Core.
- Bag rail cells match the size of the grid beside them.
- Slot styling only refreshes after something changes.
### Fixed
- Server settings are only accepted from admins, locked or not; admins listed as Steam_<id> are recognised.
- The character panel's header and resize grip no longer show over the NPC book or Dressing screen.

## 1.0.377
- The Rounded minimap setting now lives in this mod's config; your saved value is copied over.

## 1.0.376
- Worn gear always matches its equipment slot: an item that is not really worn is equipped in place or moved to your bag, and a worn item outside its slot is put back. (inventory)
- Two rings can no longer end up in the same ring slot, and equipping jewelry works again after the latest Valheim update. (fix)
- Dragging jewelry in or out of its slot, or right-clicking it off, plays the equip animation when the loot mod is installed. (jewelry)
- Hotbars start bottom left and in the order tools, ammo, food; dragging a hotbar strip works again. (UI)
- The day and time box under the minimap and the "Fires UI override", "Sky drift speed", "Minimap size" and "Minimap clock shows" settings now live in this mod's config, moved from the core mod with your values kept. "Show day and time box" keeps its name. (config)

## 1.0.368
- Fixed: hotbars jumping back to the middle of the screen when opening the inventory or map.
- Fixed "Error loading world file" on converted worlds.
- Much faster F1 hub and inventory: the stats page no longer rebuilds every second, the player and companion previews are cheaper, and other screens redraw only when something changes.
- In the classic (non-unified) UI the chest no longer overlaps the inventory grid, and dragging it no longer moves the grid.
- Works with Valheim 1.0.21.
- Security: clients only accept admin status and settings from the server, with size limits.

## 1.0.353
- Bug fixes and improvements.

## 1.0.321
- Companion grid and worn gear: click to pick an item up and drop it where you want, the same as your own inventory.
- Bag special slots: drag items between slots and swap them like vanilla, instead of them jumping to your main grid.
- Coins and currency in the coin pouch no longer add to your carry weight.
- The chest panel opens at the right size, with its title centred and space between the tabs and the grid.
- Crafting stations with extra tabs (such as the Tinker Bench) show every tab without overlapping.

## 1.0.299
- Extra hotbars: drag anywhere on a bar to move it, and resize it from the corner. Fires UI and classic UI each keep their own hotbar layout.
- Fixed the Tools hotbar turning sideways or growing too big after switching UI modes.
- Companions now get the full Stats page, same as yours. Effects say what they do, with good in green and bad in red.
- The companion panel has Gear, Stats and Appearance buttons under the armour; Appearance lets you change your companion's look.
- The chest panel no longer snaps back when you move it, keeps its size, and no longer covers your inventory.
- The item you're dragging now shows in front of every panel, and the player panel resizes from its corner again.
- Hiding Food or Ammo slots no longer hides the left ring or the teleport bypass slot, and a companion's jewelry now shows in their panel.
- Fixed blank button labels, a white trash can and broken slot borders at small screen sizes.
